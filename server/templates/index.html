Diplomacy — quick rules

The board

Europe is divided into provinces. Some are land, some sea, some coastal (have both a coast and an interior). 34 provinces are supply centers — capturing them is how you grow. The first power to 18 wins.

This variant has 6 powers (Italy is omitted; Italian centers start neutral).

Units

Each power has armies (A, on land) and fleets (F, on sea or coast). Every unit is equal strength — there is no health, no levels, no resources beyond supply centers.

Each turn — Spring then Fall

  • Negotiate — talk privately or publicly. The AIs negotiate too; you can iterate as much as you like.
  • Order — every unit gets one order: hold, move, support, or convoy.
  • Resolve — orders are revealed and adjudicated simultaneously. Equal strength = bounce, both stay home.

After Fall: supply centers update (whoever's army or fleet sits in one in Fall owns it). Then Adjustment: build a new unit at any vacant home center if you have more SCs than units; disband if fewer.

Combat — strength is just unit count

  • One unit moving in = strength 1. A defending unit = strength 1.
  • A support from an adjacent unit adds 1 to either an attack or a defense.
  • If attacker total > defender total, attacker wins and defender is dislodged.
  • Supports get cut if the supporter is itself attacked (unless from the province being supported into).
  • You can never dislodge your own units, even with overwhelming force.

Convoys

An army can move across a sea if a friendly fleet (or a chain of friendly fleets) sits in that sea and orders Convoy. Order the fleet to convoy and the army to move; both must succeed.

Dislodgement and retreats

A dislodged unit must retreat to an empty adjacent province (one that didn't bounce in this turn and isn't where the attack came from), or disband.

Talking is the whole game

Diplomacy has no dice. The only way to win is to convince other powers to support you — and to read them well enough to know when they're lying. The AIs each have hidden personalities, hidden agendas, and a memory that persists across games. Stab someone today, they'll remember next time.

Click-to-order in this UI

  • Click your unit on the map. A small popover appears with action buttons.
  • Highlighted yellow provinces are valid moves; click one.
  • For supports and convoys, the popover guides you.
  • Anything you don't order will hold automatically.
